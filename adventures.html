Ch. 6 Trail Log

Three trips, two of them with the same two friends I met on the first one. The pattern has held for three years now: pick something a little beyond what we have done before, drive to the trailhead, and find out how it goes.

September 2026 · Indian Peaks Wilderness

Junco Lake Loop

Two nights, four of us, and the most distance we had ever tried. Day one was eleven miles and 2,500 feet, which was probably ambitious, and I do not think I have ever been that sore. The views, the air, and hours offline just talking made it the best way I know to get away from everything for a while.

  • 22 miles
  • A mile of climbing
  • 2 nights
  • 4 friends

Summer 2024 · Colorado to Santa Cruz

The 3,000-Mile Move-Out

An almost 3,000-mile odyssey with the same two friends, moving one of them out of the dorms and back to Santa Cruz. Endless playlists, several full audiobooks, and a lot of talking about life. Along the way: kayaking a slot canyon in a thunderstorm, fishing one of our tents out of a lake, squeezing through a cave so tight you could not move your arms, and trying surfing for the first time.

  • ~3,000 miles
  • 3 of us
  • 1 tent recovered from a lake
  • 0 surfing lessons

October 2023 · White River National Forest

Mohawk Lakes, Fall Break

One night, three of us, fall break of freshman year. We never figured out the camping stove, so dinner was cold ramen and Oreos, and we slept on top of snow through what is still the coldest night I have had. It should not have been a good trip. It is one of my favorite memories, and it is where two of my best friends came from.

  • 1 night
  • 3 of us
  • 0 hot meals
  • 2 lifelong friends

Ch. 7 Summer Evenings

Whenever the snow lets us

Disc Golf

Easy afternoons at the local park, or a whole day on one of Colorado's mountain courses. It is the lowest-effort way I know to get a few hours outdoors with friends and still keep score.

Late summer, under the lights

Pickleball

I played tennis in high school, and pickleball scratches the same competitive itch with a low enough barrier that everyone will actually come out. We have spent a lot of evenings at the local courts with a speaker going, trading trash talk until they turn the lights off.

VP!

Mondays, 10 pm to midnight

Bowling

Never something I took seriously, until a close friend and I befriended the club president freshman year. Three years, three bowling balls, and one Vice President position later, I am at the alley every Monday night with the rest of the Mines bowling club.

  • 3 years
  • 3 balls
  • Club Vice President

Ch. 8 Powder Days

Every Saturday we can afford it

Skiing

I grew up getting one or two ski weekends a year with my family, and that turned into a real love for the snowy Rockies, cutting in and out of the trees. These days my two adventure buddies and I scrape together enough for season passes, wake up at 6 am, stuff our pockets with peanut butter and jelly sandwiches and granola bars, and sit in three hours of traffic to get our mountain fix.

  • 6 am alarms
  • 3 hours of I-70
  • PB&J in every pocket
